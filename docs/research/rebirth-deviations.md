# Rebirth deviations

> Deliberate differences from v0.8.82 that the project owner asked for. They are not research findings: the original values stay in the other KB files and in the generated defs, and the rebirth applies these on top through the defs layer in `packages/defs/src/rebirth/` (rebirth-only defs and balance deviations), so the simulation, the client and the bots read the same values. Source `user/<date>-<topic>` is the owner's request (see `sources.md`).

## Rainy matches (2026-10-08)

> The owner: 30 % of the 50v50 and vanilla matches should be a little dark with rain falling all day, start to finish; effects only (a storm event with gameplay is a far-future idea, not this). Code: `packages/defs/src/rebirth/weather.ts` (`isRainyMatch`), `apps/client/src/fx/weather.ts` (`RainFx`, run by `game/worldFx.ts`), `fx/cameraEmitters.ts` (the rain options), `fx/particles.ts` (`skip`, `cull`), `objects/world.ts` (`localRoofs`), `objects/building.ts` (`ceilingOnSegment`), `fx/particleDefsAmbient.ts` (`rain`, `rainSplash`, `rainRipple`, `falling_rain`), `render/renderer.ts` (`weather` container), the `weatherFx` setting (`config.ts`, `menu/settingsModal.ts`). Tests: `packages/defs/test/weather.test.ts`, `apps/client/test/weather.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/rain.spec.ts`.

- A match rains when its map is a classic or 50v50 map without camera particles of its own (`main`, `main_summer`, `faction`) and the weather roll of its map seed is below 0.3; every other map stays dry and keeps its own look (snow, desert, woods, the Halloween night), and so do `main_spring` (its falling blossoms) and `faction_potato` (the "Potato vs Tomato" event and its falling potatoes and tomatoes), whose particles drifting at 2–3 units/s would mix with rain falling at 30–36 [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [H]
- The roll is a salted 32-bit integer hash of the map seed (no `Math.random`): 2,988 of seeds 0–9,999 rain on `main`. Every client reads the seed from the Map message it already gets, so all players of a match see the same weather with no wire change (no protocol schema bump) and the simulation and the bots never read it [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [H]
- The look, in the flat style and light enough to keep players and loot as readable as on a dry day: a screen-space multiply tint (`0xccd6e6`) over the world, under the red zone, the screen effects and the UI, which takes about 20 % off red, 16 % off green and 10 % off blue (measured on the grass: 128/174/72 → 102/146/66); the minimap and the HUD keep their colours [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [H]
- The Halloween night's darkening is not reused: it is the biome's `valueAdjust` (0.3) multiplied into each obstacle, building, decal and particle tint when the sprite is made, with dark biome terrain colours, so it can neither fade underground, follow a setting nor tint toward blue; the rain tint is one quad, much lighter [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [src:survev/client/src/objects/obstacle.ts] [M]
- Streaks: the camera emitter of the map particles (snow, leaves) with its own def: the bullet tracer's gradient sprite (`player-bullet-trail-02`) stretched 26:1 into a streak 1.5 units long and 0.06 wide, alpha 0.5, falling at 30–36 units/s steeply down-right for 0.38–0.46 s; they spawn in the screen grown by their 14-unit fall and offset upwind by half of it (instead of survev's circle of 2.5 zoom radii), with the spawn rate scaled by that area as survev scales it by the circle's, so the density per world area is the same at every scope (about 100 streaks on a 16:9 screen at the 1x scope) up to 240 alive at once, which thins the rain from the 4x scope on [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [src:survev/client/src/map.ts] [H]
- The streaks fade out (6 per second) and stop spawning off the ground floor (bunkers, basements, stairs). Under a roof only the covered area stays dry: no streak is spawned whose fall (its longest, 16.6 units, plus half its length) would cross the zoom regions of the roof the player stands under (whose interior is drawn see-through then), and the streaks already over it are removed as the player walks in, while the rain keeps falling around the house at full strength; the tint stays indoors (a dim day through the windows) and fades out underground [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [H]
- Rings where drops land: about 28 a second per 1,000 square units in view, at most 80 a second, only in the open (never under a roof) and only while the player is on the ground floor: a small ring on the ground (the player ripple sprite, 0.3–0.4 s) or a spreading ripple on water (0.8 s). The surface is the client's floor-aware ground query (the footsteps' `groundSurface`: decals, then building floors, then rivers, lakes and the sea), so a bridge deck or a dock over a river gets ground rings, not ripples, and each water gets its own ripple colour (`lakeWaterRipple` on lakes, a water decal's own) [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [src:survev/client/src/map.ts] [H]
- Load: the streak emitter keeps its own budget of 240 particles outside the particle system's cap of 768, so rain never pushes out casings or blood, and the rings hold back when fewer than 96 places are left before that cap; `tests/e2e/rain.spec.ts` compares the frame time of an 80-player fight with the rain off and on (software WebGL: about +1 ms, the tint itself about 0.1 ms) [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [H]
- No sound: the original has no rain recording (its ambience is wind, waves, a river stream, steam and a lab hum) and no new audio is added [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [H]
- The client setting "Weather effects" (`weatherFx`, on by default; ko "날씨 효과") turns the streaks, the rings and the tint off. The loopback sandbox takes `?rain=1` (rain on any map) and `?rain=0` (dry) for testing; network games always follow the seed [src:user/2026-10-08-rain] [H]

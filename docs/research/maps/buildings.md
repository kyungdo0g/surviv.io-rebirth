# Buildings and structures

> Every building and structure def in survev `shared/defs/mapObjects/buildings/*.ts` (base, mode, bunker and cache defs) and `shared/defs/mapObjects/structureDefs.ts`, grouped by family.
> Tables are generated from the survev defs at c6185e31 and compared child-by-child with the original v0.8.82 client defs extracted from the 2026 relaunch bundle (`kong/relaunch-client-defs`).
> "spawns" lists survev's map generation (`shared/defs/maps/*`, server-only, a reconstruction): `n` fixed count, `s/l` solo-duo/squad count, `p=x` spawn odds, `dens n` density spawn, `rand 2-of-3` random rotation, `+place` place spawn, `centre` location spawn, `important` important spawn. `pre-fork:` gives survev's own pre-fork snapshot `ae55c9a8` (2025-07-01) where it differs; that snapshot is the best available original baseline for server-side spawn counts (see provenance/balance-revert.md).
> Sizes are in game units: `bounds` = `mapObstacleBounds` (the area kept free of random obstacles), otherwise the union of floor surfaces or ground patches.
> Bunkers are only indexed here; layouts, entrances and loot are in bunkers.md. Codes, buttons and recorders are in puzzles.md.

## How buildings work

### Definition fields

- A building def has `terrain`, optional `ori`/`oris`, `mapObstacleBounds`, minimap `map{display,color,shapes,displayType}`, `floor{surfaces,imgs}`, `ceiling{zoomRegions,vision,imgs,damage,destroy}`, `mapObjects` (children), and optional `occupiedEmitters`, `puzzle`, `mapGroundPatches`, `bridgeLandBounds`/`bridgeWaterBounds`, `goreRegion`, `soundEmitters`, `healRegions`, `teamId`, `zIdx` [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/buildings/buildingDefs.ts:10-136] [H]
- A structure def stacks buildings into layers (`layers[0]` ground, `layers[1]` underground), adds `stairs` (collision box, `downDir`, `noCeilingReveal`, `lootOnly`), `mask` boxes that hide the surface while underground, and an optional `interiorSound` [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/structureDefs.ts:6-40] [H]
- Layer numbers: 0 ground, 1 underground, 2 ground-side stairs, 3 underground-side stairs; two layers interact when their low bits match or both are stairs layers [src:survev/shared/utils/util.ts:47-60] [H]
- Bridge stairs are `lootOnly`: loot pushed by the river passes under bridges on the stairs layer while players cannot [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/structureDefs.ts:80-200] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/loot.ts:444] [src:wikigg/Buildings] [H]

### Spawning a building

- `genBuilding` places every child at `pos` rotated by the building orientation; a child given as a weighted record is resolved with `weightedRandomObject` and an empty-string result spawns nothing; child orientation is `(child.ori + ori) % 4` unless `inheritOri` is false [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1955-2030] [H]
- `genAuto` swaps a type through the map's `spawnReplacements` unless the child sets `ignoreMapSpawnReplacement` (e.g. `warehouse_01` keeps plain crates) [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1154-1172] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/buildings/baseBuildingDefs.ts:9756-9761] [H]
- A loot-spawner child rolls each listed tier once (min/max ignored), drops the item with push speed 0 and reserves a radius-3 circle [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1196-1212] [H]
- Buildings with `map.display` add a minimap entry only on layer 0, using `displayType` when set (caches show as a plain stone, tree or barrel) [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1982-1990] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/buildings/cacheDefs.ts:5-40] [H]
- Structures pick `ori ?? def.ori ?? random 0-3` and generate each layer building at the same position on layers 0 and 1 [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:2031-2060] [H]
- Map gen dispatches placement by `terrain`: `waterEdge` (huts, docks, conch bunker), `bridge` (fisherman's shack 03a, crossing bunker, river town), `nearbyRiver` (cabins), `riverShore`, `river` (river cache), `lakeCenter`; `spawnPriority` orders large complexes first [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1136] [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1255] [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1464-1500] [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1708-1722] [H]
- Bridges: medium when 4 < river width < 9, large when 8 < width < 20, otherwise extra-large; at most ceil(max × points/33) per river with max medium 3, large 2, xlarge 0 (50v50 places its xlarge bridges separately) [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:819-855] [H]
- River cabins: 1 cabin plus 1-2 per river at least 16 wide and 0-1 per river at least 6 wide, clamped to 1-3; each tries a `dock_01` on rivers at least 8 wide [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1047-1062] [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:1814-1890] [H]
- Random building rotation: since 0.7.7 one of bank, mansion and police station does not spawn on the normal map (survev: `randomSpawns` choose 2 of `mansion_structure_01`, `police_01`, `bank_01`) [src:changelog/0.7.7] [src:survev/shared/defs/maps/baseDefs.ts:948-953] [src:fandom/Building_Rotation] [H]

### Floors, roofs and vision

- Floor surface types used by buildings: house, shack, tile, warehouse, asphalt, container, bunker, water, grass, carpet, stone, brick, snow; they select footstep sounds, and a building `water` surface slows players by `waterSpeedPenalty` 3 [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:2378-2420] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:4726-4731] [src:survev/shared/gameConfig.ts:202] [H]
- Indoors: a player whose circle overlaps any `zoomIn` box is indoors and gets the 1x zoom radius (28 desktop / 32 mobile) or the region's `zoom`; a `zoomOut` box keeps that zoom until the player leaves it (hysteresis); `noZoom` regions count as indoors without changing zoom [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:2105-2270] [src:survev/shared/gameConfig.ts:399-412] [H]
- Region zooms in use: 48 (= 4x radius) over the bathhouse pool and 36 (= 2x) in parts of the fork Reserve; wikis confirm the 4x view at the club pool and 1x-free vision in the hunting perch [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/buildings/baseBuildingDefs.ts:3335] [src:fandom/Crimson_Ring_Club] [src:fandom/Hunting_Perch] [src:wikigg/The_Reserve] [H]
- Roof hiding (client): the roof fades out while a ray scan from the player reaches a `zoomIn` box within `vision.dist` (default 5.5) and `width` (default 2.75); it lingers `linger` seconds (default 0) and fades back at `fadeRate` (default 12/s) [src:survev/client/src/objects/building.ts:262-275] [src:survev/client/src/objects/building.ts:463-520] [H]
- Roof destruction: `ceiling.destroy.wallCount` breakable walls destroyed ⇒ the roof is removed for good and a residue sprite drawn (huts/outhouses 2, teahouse 3, greenhouse 7, perch 5); `ceiling.damage` marks the roof damaged when an obstacle with `damageCeiling` (stoves) is destroyed [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/building.ts:335-352] [src:survev/client/src/objects/building.ts:437-462] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/obstacles/furnitureDefs.ts:1141-1170] [H]
- Occupied emitters (chimney smoke on cabin, saloon, reserve; steam in the bathhouse; camp smoke) run while any living player on the same layer overlaps a `zoomIn` box, and stop forever once an obstacle with `disableBuildingOccupied` (the stove) is destroyed [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/building.ts:293-330] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/building.ts:343-346] [src:survev/client/src/objects/building.ts:522-526] [H]
- Wikis describe the same tell: the cabin chimney smokes while someone is inside unless the stove is destroyed [src:fandom/Cabin] [src:fandom/Saloon] [M]
- Heal regions add `healRate` HP/s while standing inside (not when downed, not in the gas): bathhouse saunas 3, camp fire 2 (fork), oasis 1 (fork) [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:2143-2165] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/buildings/baseBuildingDefs.ts:3865] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/buildings/modeBuildingDefs.ts:32] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/buildings/modeBuildingDefs.ts:995] [H]
- Gore regions: each death inside the region increments `goreKills` on the building's gore decals; the club pool turns fully red at 4 kills and the club logo decal fades in between 4 and 6 kills [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:3005-3020] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/building.ts:477-485] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/decalDefs.ts:522-575] [H]
- Fandom describes the same pool behaviour (fully red after 4 kills; Eye, club and PARMA decals after 6) [src:fandom/Crimson_Ring_Club] [M]

## Common mechanics

- Every bunker is a two-layer structure: the surface building on layer 0 and the sublevel on layer 1, joined by stairs; `mask` boxes hide the surface while the player is underground [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/structureDefs.ts:6-40] [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:2031-2060] [H]
- Bunker walls are metal and reflect bullets; most bunkers show their logo; the Eye bunker has no logo over its entrance [src:fandom/Bunkers] [src:wikigg/Bunkers] [src:fandom/Eye_Bunker] [M]
- Stair-bottom doors are `house_door_02` (indestructible, open by hand); inner doors are sliding `lab_door_01` that open automatically within 2 units and close 1 s after the last player leaves; `lab_door_02`/`03` open only from one side (`openOneWay` 1) [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/obstacles/buildingObjsDefs.ts:249] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/obstacles/buildingObjsDefs.ts:323-342] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:2209-2232] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/obstacle.ts:236-260] [H]
- Changelog 0.3.6 added sliding doors, vats, computer terminals, power boxes and a reverb filter underground; 0.6.2 made all doors open automatically [src:changelog/0.3.6] [src:changelog/0.6.2] [H]
- Explosive props: `control_panel_03` (computer terminal, 150 HP), `control_panel_06` (200 HP), `power_box_01` and `barrel_01` explode with `explosion_barrel`; fire extinguishers `fire_ext_01` release smoke [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/obstacles/furnitureDefs.ts:1140] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/obstacles/interactableDefs.ts:1] [src:fandom/Conch_Bunker] [H]
- Common crates: bunker crate `crate_08` (tier_surviv 2-3), gold bunker crate `crate_07`, conch crate `crate_09` (tier_chest 1-2 + tier_conch), hatchet case `case_03` (tier_hatchet), ammo crate `crate_04`, small ammo crate `crate_06` (armor-plated, tier_ammo), locker `locker_01` (tier_world, 20 HP), Initiative locker `locker_03` [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/obstacles/crateDefs.ts:1] [src:survev/shared/defs/mapObjects/obstacles/furnitureDefs.ts:1] [src:kong/relaunch-client-defs] [H]
- Woods replaces bunker crates, conch crates and soviet crates with hatchet crates `crate_19` (tier_guns 1-3 + tier_surviv 2-3) through `spawnReplacements` [src:survev/shared/defs/maps/woodsDefs.ts:255-265] [src:fandom/Storm_Bunker] [H]
- Water floors (`water` surface in the conch and crossing main rooms) slow players by 3 in survev [src:survev/server/src/game/map.ts:2378-2420] [src:survev/shared/gameConfig.ts:202] [H]

# Roles

> Every role def in survev `shared/defs/gameObjects/roleDefs.ts` (19 ids): 50v50 faction roles, map roles (Woods King, Kill Leader, The Hunted) and Cobalt classes, with their perks, starting gear per team, sounds, announcement/kill-feed flags and the promotion rules in the survev server.
> The original client role defs (survev commit `9f64948d`, `src/defs/roleDefs.js`, cited `derived/survev@9f64948d:...`) only carry announce, killFeed, sound, mapIcon, perks, mapIndicator, visor, GUI image and colour. Starting items and promotion timing were server-side; survev's `defaultItems` and timings are reconstructions, later changed by the fork.
> survev's pre-fork reconstruction (commit `172a4348`, 2025-04-29, the last version before the fork's Captain/role-item changes) is cited `derived/survev@172a4348:shared/defs/gameObjects/roleDefs.ts:<line>`. Perk effects are in perks.md, helmet stats in gear.md.

## 50v50 promotion rules

- Faction roles are scheduled per gas circle (`gameConfig.roles.timings`, each `{role, circleIdx, wait}`); when a timer runs out, one random eligible player per team is promoted [src:survev/shared/defs/mapDefs.ts:160-167] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:444-463] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:320-375] [H]
- Eligible: alive, connected, not downed and without a role; a role decided by a function is rolled once and given to both teams [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:328-332] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:455-461] [H]
- Fork 0.3.1 AFK filter: players alive over 5 s who stood still more than 50 % of the time, or did not move in the last 5 s, are skipped unless nobody else is eligible; only the first connected join of an account counts (fork) [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:333-371] [src:survev/client/public/changelogRec.html:168] [H]
- v0.8.82 order (fandom): the Commander first, then one of Lieutenant, Marksman, Recon or Grenadier ("not all four"), then a Medic and a Bugler [src:fandom/Lieutenant] [src:fandom/Marksman] [src:fandom/Recon] [src:fandom/Grenadier] [src:fandom/Medic] [M]
- Before 0.8.4 two Lieutenants were promoted per team; 0.8.4 cut it to one; from 0.8.81 the Lieutenant does not appear every game [src:changelog/0.8.4] [src:fandom/Lieutenant] [H]
- survev pre-fork timings (commit 42f052d8, 2024-09-22): Commander 50 s, random Lieutenant/Marksman/Recon/Grenadier 54 s, Medic 58 s, Bugler 62 s after gas circle 0 starts (the first version, a7a91a25, used 5/10/15/20 s) [src:derived/survev-git-42f052d8] [src:derived/survev-git-a7a91a25] [L]
- Fork (v0.0.18, commit 4a45bce2): every role is given out, in the order Commander 50 s, Lieutenant 54, Marksman 58, Recon 62, Grenadier 66, Medic 70, Bugler 74 (fork) [src:survev/shared/defs/maps/factionDefs.ts:194-240] [src:survev/client/public/changelogRec.html:509] [src:derived/survev-git-4a45bce2] [H]
- Fandom: a player with a bad connection ("bad transmission") is never chosen as Commander [src:fandom/Commander] [L]

## What promotion does

- On promotion the server broadcasts a `RoleAnnouncement` (role, assigned, playerId); the client plays the assign sound (in Cobalt only for the local player, except Kill Leader), adds "<name> promoted to <role>!" to the kill feed when `killFeed.assign`, and shows "You've been promoted to <role>!" to the promoted player when `announce` [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:913-918] [src:survev/client/src/game.ts:1413-1470] [src:survev/client/src/ui/ui2.ts:1513-1536] [src:survev/client/src/en.json:735-737] [H]
- When a role holder dies: "<killer> killed <role>!" or "<role> is dead!" in the kill feed if `killFeed.dead`, plus the dead sound; kill-feed colour is `killFeed.color` or the team colour [src:survev/client/src/game.ts:1471-1500] [src:survev/client/src/ui/ui2.ts:1505-1548] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:2849-2858] [H]
- Halloween (`spookyKillSounds`) replaces the Kill Leader assign/dead sounds with the `kill_leader_assigned_01/02` and `kill_leader_dead_01/02` groups [src:survev/client/src/game.ts:1423-1432] [src:fandom/Kill_Leader] [H]
- Switching roles removes the old role's no-drop helmet and chest and its role perks; a loot perk that the new role also grants is dropped [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:890-900] [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:965-984] [H]
- `defaultItems` are applied in order: backpack (old one dropped), inventory items (excess dropped), outfit (old one dropped unless it is no-drop or the loadout outfit), role helmet (replacing a non-role helmet, which drops), chest (old one dropped), then weapons [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:986-1082] [H]
- Role guns replace the slot's gun (old one dropped); `fillInv` fills that gun's ammo to backpack capacity; an empty slot entry instead refills the existing gun's magazine [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:1048-1080] [H]
- Team-dependent items use the team id (Red 1, Blue 2; non-faction modes alternate) [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:987-988] [src:survev/shared/defs/gameObjects/roleDefs.ts:17-29] [H]
- In 50v50 a player with a role whose outfit is no-drop cannot pick up outfits [src:survev/server/src/game/objects/player.ts:3904-3911] [H]
- Fandom: promotion auto-selects the role's scope unless a better scope (15x) is selected [src:fandom/Commander] [M]

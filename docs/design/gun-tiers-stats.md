# Gun tier stats (generated)

Generated by `tools/research/gun-tiers/report.ts` from `compute.ts` (method: [gun-tiers.md](gun-tiers.md)). Final defs: `@rebirth/defs` `getDefOfType("gun", id)` with the rebirth layer applied. CSV twin: [gun-tiers-stats.csv](gun-tiers-stats.csv).

Columns:

- **dmg**: bullet damage x pellets, + explosion (e) + shrapnel pieces x damage (s).
- **RPM**: average rate (bursts and pumps included). **mag**: magazine (extended).
- **reload**: seconds (full magazine), or seconds per shell batch (full reload).
- **burst / sust. DPS**: damage per second at the cyclic rate / including the reload (a charge gun: per pickup).
- **spread**: standing / moving (degrees). **speed**: bullet or projectile u/s.
- **TTK avg**: expected seconds from the first shot at average aim (3.5°), mean over the class band, armour none / 1 / 2.
- **exp A1**: expert aim (1.6°) at level 1. **perf A1**: every bullet hits (15 % headshots).
- **F**: forgiveness, expert / beginner band TTK at level 1, fixed aim error. **mob**: speed held / firing (u/s).
- **tier**: current → proposed (`=` unchanged).
